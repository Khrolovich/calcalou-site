Questions, answered.

Is Calcalou free?

Yes. Logging meals and activity, goals, streaks, levels, achievements and stats are free. Calcalou Premium unlocks AI photo scans, up to 50 a day, and removes banner ads. It starts with a 7-day free trial.

Who is Lou?

Lou is the little character who lives in the app. Lou reacts to what you log, celebrates your streaks and gently nudges you when you skip a day.

How accurate are photo estimates?

Photo scans give a quick estimate, not a lab measurement. All calorie figures in Calcalou are estimates. It is a wellness app, not a medical device: talk to a doctor or a registered dietitian before making big changes to your diet.

Which devices does it work on?

iPhone, iPad and Android phones. Sign in with Apple or Google on iPhone and iPad, with Google or email on Android, and your diary, streaks and levels sync between devices.

What happens to my data?

Your food log is your data and is never sold. You can delete your account and all its data at any time. How to delete your account
