Questions, answered.

Is Calcalou free?

Yes. Logging calories and protein, barcode scanning, water, goals, streaks, levels, achievements and stats are free. Calcalou Premium unlocks AI photo scans, up to 50 a day, and removes ads. Where offered, an account can try up to 3 free scans once.

Can Calcalou track protein?

Yes. Log protein with every entry, set a daily goal from your body weight or enter your own, and see it next to calories. AI photo scans estimate protein, fat and carbs for each food; barcodes and manual entries are free. Estimates can be wrong, so check labels when it matters.

Who is Lou?

Lou is the little character who lives in the app. Lou reacts to what you log, celebrates your streaks and gently nudges you when you skip a day.

How accurate are photo estimates?

Photo scans give a quick estimate, not a lab measurement. All calorie figures in Calcalou are estimates. It is a wellness app, not a medical device: talk to a doctor or a registered dietitian before making big changes to your diet.

Which devices does it work on?

iPhone, iPad and Android phones. Continue with Apple (iPhone and iPad), Google or email, and your diary, streaks and levels sync between devices.

What happens to my data?

Your food log is your data and is never sold. You can delete your account and all its data at any time. How to delete your account
