How to choose the best protein tracker for you

The best protein tracker is one that fits how you log food. Compare free manual entry, barcodes, editable photo estimates, protein goals and nutrient detail.

This guide describes version 1.7. Store rollout may differ by platform and country; check the version available to you before downloading for a specific feature.

Written by the Calcalou Team. This is a product guide, not an independent test or an accuracy ranking. Competitor information was checked against the linked official sources on 17 November 2026. Plans, languages and availability can change.

Start with the foods you actually eat

Test your usual breakfast, a packaged snack and a home-cooked meal. Check the label, serving size and protein units. A large database is useful only if the entries you use are complete and correct.

Keep protein visible beside calories

Look for a daily protein target in grams and a clear daily total alongside calories. Calcalou 1.7 can suggest a protein goal from body weight. A target is a starting point for tracking, not a personal medical recommendation.

Decide what must be free

Calcalou 1.7 offers free manual calorie and protein logging and free barcodes. Cronometer Basic also has free barcodes and adds detailed vitamins and minerals. MyFitnessPal's official plan guide puts barcode scanning and gram-based macro targets in Premium. Check the current plan in your country.

Use photos as editable estimates

Photo logging can reduce typing, but it cannot reliably see every ingredient or portion. Calcalou 1.7 and Cal AI describe photo macro estimates; Lose It! Snap It suggests food database matches. Correct the result, and enter missing macros yourself instead of counting them as zero.

Choose by the job you need done

Try Calcalou 1.7 for a daily calorie and protein routine with Lou, water and widgets. Try Cronometer when detailed nutrients matter most. Consider MyFitnessPal for its diary, integrations and paid planning tools, or Lose It! and Cal AI if you prefer their logging flow. This guide awards no universal winner.

Check language, age and availability

Calcalou 1.7 has 41 interface languages. The app is 13+ subject to local requirements; AI scans and Premium purchases in 1.7 are 18+ and region-dependent. Sign-in is required. Verify the store version before relying on a feature.

Questions about Calcalou

What should I check in a protein tracker?

Check daily protein grams, an editable target, portion controls, barcode data quality, clear missing values and the cost of the logging tools you will use.

Is an AI photo a measurement of protein?

No. It is an estimate. Portion size, ingredients and food preparation affect the result. Verify labels or weighed ingredients when accuracy matters.

Does free barcode scanning make Calcalou unique?

No. Calcalou and Cronometer Basic both offer it. Choose using your whole workflow, not one feature.
