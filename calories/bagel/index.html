Why your number may differ

This is a plain bagel; onion, poppy seed and sesame bagels have their own values. Toasting only removes water, so the calories per bagel stay the same even though the value per 100 g rises. Cream cheese, butter and fillings are extra, and bagels vary in size between bakeries.
