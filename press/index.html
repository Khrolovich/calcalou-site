About Calcalou

Most calorie counters feel like spreadsheets. Calcalou feels like a game you want to come back to. Log a meal by hand in seconds, or snap a photo and get an AI calorie estimate. See what's left for today, with calories burned counted in. Lou reacts to what you log, celebrates streaks and nudges you when you skip a day.

  • Eaten and burned calories in one place, with a daily goal to lose, keep or gain weight
  • Daily streaks, XP, levels, achievements and daily and weekly challenges
  • Weekly summary and stats
  • AI photo calorie estimates (Premium)
  • Apple Health and Health Connect: adds your daily calories and shows your active energy
  • Home and Lock Screen widgets, a Live Activity and Siri Shortcuts on iPhone
  • Cloud sync across devices

Calcalou is a food and calorie logging app for general wellness, not a medical device. Calorie figures, including AI photo estimates, are estimates.
