About Calcalou

Calcalou puts protein right beside calories. Set a daily protein goal from your body weight, scan a barcode or type a meal for free, or snap a photo with Premium and get an AI estimate of calories, protein, fat and carbs for each food. Lou reacts to what you log, celebrates streaks and nudges you when you skip a day.

  • Calories and protein side by side on Today, with a protein goal from your body weight (0.8 to 2.0 g/kg) or one you set yourself
  • Free barcode scanning and manual logging of calories and protein
  • AI photo estimates for each food on the plate (Premium, up to 50 a day, 18+)
  • Daily streaks, XP, levels and achievements
  • Water tracking, several reminders with their own times and days, CSV export by period
  • Apple Health and Health Connect
  • Home and Lock Screen widgets that show calories or protein
  • Cloud sync across devices

Calcalou is a food and calorie logging app for general wellness, not a medical device. Calorie and protein figures, including AI photo estimates, are estimates.
