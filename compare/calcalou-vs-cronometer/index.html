Calcalou vs Cronometer: calories and protein

Calcalou 1.7 focuses on calories, protein and a habit with Lou. Cronometer fits better for detailed vitamin and nutrient records. Both have free barcodes.

This guide describes version 1.7. Store rollout may differ by platform and country; check the version available to you before downloading for a specific feature.

Written by the Calcalou Team. This is a product guide, not an independent test or an accuracy ranking. Competitor information was checked against the linked official sources on 17 November 2026. Plans, languages and availability can change.

Which fits your routine?

Consider Calcalou 1.7 if you want calories and protein together, water tracking and a friendly daily routine. Choose Cronometer if detailed micronutrient analysis is central to your decision. Free barcodes are not unique to Calcalou: Cronometer Basic includes them too.

Review the result

Food data and photos can be incomplete. Check portions and nutrition labels. Do not treat a missing macro as zero. A logging app is not a medical device.
