# Calcalou Support

**Last updated:** October 7, 2026

## Contact us

Write to **[support@calcalou.com](mailto:support@calcalou.com?subject=Calcalou%20support)** —
that is a real inbox, read by the developer. We reply within two business days.

To get a faster answer, tell us:

- your device and iOS/Android version,
- the app version (Profile → bottom of the screen),
- what you did and what happened instead.

## Common questions

### How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by the store, not by us.

- **iPhone / iPad:** Settings → tap your name → Subscriptions → Calcalou → Cancel.
- **Android:** Play Store → profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions →
  Calcalou → Cancel.

Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date. A free trial turns into a paid
subscription unless you cancel at least 24 hours before it ends.

### I paid but Premium is not active

Open the paywall and tap **Restore Purchases**, making sure you are signed in with the
Apple ID or Google account you bought with. If it still does not appear, email us the
store receipt and we will sort it out.

### How do I ask for a refund?

Refunds are handled by the store. For the App Store, use
[reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com). For Google Play, use the
[Play Store refund form](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637).

### How do I delete my account and my data?

In the app: **Profile → Delete Account**. It removes your entries, settings and progress
from our database and from the device.

Prefer email? Write to
[support@calcalou.com](mailto:support@calcalou.com?subject=Delete%20my%20Calcalou%20account)
from the address on the account and we will delete it within 30 days.

### How do I turn off ads?

A Premium subscription removes banner ads. If you are in the EEA or the UK you can also
change how ads are personalised at **Profile → Ad Privacy Options**; on iOS, tracking is
controlled in **Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking**.

### How accurate is the AI photo scan?

It is an estimate, not a measurement. Calcalou tells you how confident it is, and you can
edit every number before saving. Calcalou is not a medical device and does not give
medical or dietary advice — talk to a professional about anything health-related.

### Something else

Bugs, feature ideas and complaints are all welcome at
**[support@calcalou.com](mailto:support@calcalou.com)**.
