What is Calcalou? Calorie and protein tracker

Calcalou is a calorie and protein tracker for iPhone, iPad and Android, with Lou, a friendly mascot that helps make daily logging a habit.

This guide describes version 1.7. Store rollout may differ by platform and country; check the version available to you before downloading for a specific feature.

What Calcalou does

  • Log calories and protein manually for free.
  • Scan product barcodes for free. Nutrition values depend on the product data available; check the label and portion size.
  • Set a protein goal from your body weight: Calcalou shows the calculation (weight × grams per kilogram), lets you adjust it and accepts your own number.
  • Estimate a meal from a photo with AI, including calories, protein, fat and carbs for each food when available. Review and edit the result before saving.
  • Add water by the glass or as a custom amount in ml.
  • Set several reminders, each with its own time and days of the week.
  • Keep a logging streak with Lou and follow today's calories or protein in a widget on your Home Screen. Widget features differ by platform.
  • Export your diary as a CSV for a month, quarter, year or all time.
  • Use the app in 41 interface languages in version 1.7. Sign-in is required to keep your diary in your account.

Questions about Calcalou

Is Calcalou free?

Manual calorie and protein logging, barcode scanning and water tracking are free. AI scans are Premium; where offered, an account can try up to 3 free scans once. Subscription prices and eligibility are shown in the app and store.

Does a photo scan count protein?

In version 1.7, AI photo scans can estimate calories, protein, fat and carbs for each detected food. Estimates can be wrong, especially for portions, oils and mixed dishes. Missing nutrition values are not zero: check or enter them manually.

Who can use Calcalou?

Calcalou is for ages 13 and up, subject to local age requirements. In version 1.7, AI scans and Premium purchases require age 18 or above and an eligible region. Calcalou is a general wellness app, not a medical device.

Can I use it without signing in?

Sign-in is required. Your diary is associated with your account; use the same sign-in method when returning on another device.
